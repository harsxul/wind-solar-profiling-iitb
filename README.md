# Wind and solar profiling of the IIT Bombay campus (PH303 Supervised Learning Project, 2023-24)

Supervisor: Prof. M. Aslam (Physics); co-supervisors Prof. Rajkumar S. Pant (Aerospace) and Prof. Siddhartha P. Duttagupta (EE).

The project mapped wind speeds and solar irradiance across campus rooftops, built contour maps of both, and evaluated
candidate sites for a small wind turbine and a PV installation. It grew out of the LTA Systems Lab aerostat project.

`PV_module_model.slx` is the MATLAB/Simulink photovoltaic-module model used for the solar yield estimates.
The report and presentations are available on request.

Harshul Bhatt, B.Tech Engineering Physics, IIT Bombay (2021-2025).
